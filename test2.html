Mao's Braised Pork
毛氏红烧肉
Mao Zedong's Hunan hometown dish — thick-cut pork belly braised in soy, sugar and chili until meltingly tender. Rich, sticky, and famous across China.
¥40–60/portion
🐟
Sour Broth Fish
酸汤鱼
Fish simmered in a tangy fermented broth with chili and tomato. A Hunan/Guizhou border staple — bright, sour, and spicy.
¥50–80City restaurants
🥩
Smoked Pork
腊肉
Tujia minority specialty — pork smoked over wood and stir-fried with garlic shoots or chili. Deep smoky flavour, very different from BBQ.
¥30–50Local specialty

Hunan Hotpot
湖南火锅
Spicier than Chongqing-style. Numbing mala broth loaded with chili oil. Perfect for a group — order offal, tofu, and veggies to go with the meat.
¥60–100/person
🌿
Stir-fried Wild Vegetables
野菜炒肉
Foraged mountain greens from the Wulingyuan area stir-fried with pork. Simple but tastes completely different from city vegetables.
¥25–40Near park / hotel
