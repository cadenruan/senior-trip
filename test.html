Inside the Park
🚡
Bailong Elevator (百龙天梯)
World's tallest outdoor elevator — 326m up a cliff face. Included with park entry. Go early to avoid queues.
🚠
Cable Cars
Multiple cable car routes across the park. Tianmen Mountain has the longest cable car in the world (~7.5 km).
🚌
Free Shuttle Buses
Included with park ticket. Connect all major areas. Pick up a route map at the gate.
