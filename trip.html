Good to Know
Before You Go
Japan: Get Suica cards at NRT airport. Cash is king at markets.

China: Download WeChat & Alipay before you fly — nearly everything is cashless. VPN recommended for Google/Instagram.

Korea: T-money card for transit. Most places accept cards.

All China cities: Carry your passport everywhere — hotels, attractions, and trains require it.

First Thing
Get a Suica Card at NRT
At Narita Airport, get a Suica IC card at the JR East Welcome Service counter (Terminal 2) or any ticket machine. Load ¥3,000–5,000 on it. Tap in and out of every train, subway, and bus — and use it at convenience stores.
Airport to City
Narita Express (N'EX)
~¥3,000 · ~90 minutes to Shinjuku/Shibuya. Direct, comfortable, luggage-friendly. Buy at airport JR ticket windows — there's a ¥4,000 round-trip tourist discount.
Getting Around
🚇
Tokyo Metro + JR Lines
Suica card works on all lines. Use Google Maps for directions — enter in English, it handles everything. Most rides ¥170–320.
🚌
Buses
Suica works here too. Less useful than trains for tourists but good for Asakusa area.
🚕
Taxi / Uber
Uber works in Tokyo. Taxis are safe but expensive — ¥700 flag fall + meter. Use for late nights only.
Airport Departure
⚠️ Jun 7 · 8:55 AM Flight
Leave hotel by 5:30–6:00 AM. Narita is ~90 min from central Tokyo on N'EX. Arrive airport ~7:15 AM minimum for international departure. Set multiple alarms the night before.

Don't Miss
Chongqing Hot Pot
Chongqing is the birthplace of mala hot pot. Extremely spicy numbing broth (málà). Start with medium spice if you're not used to it. Local chains: Dezhuang (德庄), Qin Ma (秦妈).
🌶️
Add More Food Spots
Xiaomian noodles, ciba (sticky rice cake), and street food in Jiefangbei.
Key Info
WeChat Pay / Alipay Required
China is nearly cashless. Set up WeChat Pay or Alipay before you arrive — you'll need them for transit, food, and shopping. Both support foreign cards now. Also set up a VPN before entering China for Google/Instagram access.
Getting Around
🚇
Chongqing Rail Transit
Metro system covers the city well. The famous Line 2 passes through a residential building (Liziba station) — worth seeing. Pay with WeChat/Alipay QR code.
🚕
DiDi (Chinese Uber)
Download DiDi app. Works with WeChat Pay. Most drivers speak no English — use the in-app translation or show your destination on maps in Chinese characters.

Must-Do
Avatar Mountains (Hallelujah Mountain)
The real-world inspiration for Pandora in Avatar. Take the Bailong Elevator (世界最高户外电梯) — world's tallest outdoor elevator. Tianmen Mountain and the glass-bottom skywalk are also incredible.
🏔️
Add Day-by-Day Plan
3 nights, Jun 14–17. Plan which trails, cable cars, and viewpoints to hit each day.
🍲
Add Food Spots
Hunan cuisine is famously spicy — try local specialties around the park area.
Getting There from Chongqing
Flight or high-speed train (~4–5 hrs). Zhangjiajie Hehua Airport (DYG) is small — check connections carefully. DiDi works for local transport within the city to the park.
Park Transport
🚡
Bailong Elevator
World's tallest outdoor elevator — 326m up the cliff face. Included with park entrance ticket. Lines can be long — go early.
🚌
Park Shuttle Buses
Free buses connect major areas within Zhangjiajie National Forest Park. Pick up a route map at the gate.

The Big One
Terracotta Warriors
One of the greatest archaeological sites in the world — 8,000+ life-sized clay soldiers. About 1 hr from city center by bus or DiDi. Get there early (8 AM) before tour groups. Allow 3–4 hours minimum.
Also Must-Do
City Wall (城墙): Rent bikes and ride the top of the ancient 14km city wall.

Muslim Quarter (回民街): The most incredible street food market. Go hungry.
